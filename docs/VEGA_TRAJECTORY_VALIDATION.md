# Vega 2014 Trajectory Validation

This document records a forensic validation of the reconstructed atmospheric trajectory against the Vega results reported in Chapter 6 of the 2014 MSc thesis.

The purpose is **not** to tune the reconstruction until it reproduces the dissertation. Surviving development files are incomplete and the original `RocketDynEq.m` has not been recovered. Differences are therefore treated as evidence about missing implementation details.

## Historical targets

The thesis reports for the Vega validation:

- vertical ascent followed by gravity turn starting at **500 m**;
- transition from gravity turn to free flight at **97.1 s**;
- transition criterion described in the algorithm as **Kn = 5**;
- total flight time **357.4 s**;
- maximum dynamic pressure at approximately **9 km altitude**.

Table 6.1 supplies the launcher masses, thrust, burn time, Isp and diameters used as the published reference data.

## Reconstructed thesis-mode model

`simulate_thesis_2014_atmospheric_phase.m` is intentionally separated from the modern spherical-Earth trajectory. It uses:

- thesis gravity-turn equations (3.1)-(3.4);
- a non-rotating Earth;
- vertical flight to 500 m followed by a 89.5 degree flight-path-angle seed;
- local gravity from Eq. (3.55) as printed;
- the thesis Eq. (3.49) `Cd(Mach)` polynomial;
- the independently reconstructed extended atmosphere;
- propellant mass flow from documented propellant mass / burn time;
- a Knudsen event based on the last-stage characteristic length.

The missing `RocketDynEq.m` means that the exact original switch logic cannot presently be reproduced.

## Results

Results below were generated by MATLAB GitHub Actions from the public reconstruction.

| Input interpretation | Transition criterion | Transition time | Transition altitude | max-q altitude | Error vs 97.1 s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Thesis Table 6.1, active P80 drag diameter | Kn=5 | 142.413 s | 128.8 km | 9.81 km | +45.313 s |
| Thesis masses, recovered 1.9 m aerodynamic diameter | Kn=5 | 140.957 s | 128.8 km | 9.98 km | +43.857 s |
| Table 6.4 first-stage m0 + recovered 1.9 m diameter | Kn=5 | 138.085 s | 128.8 km | 10.01 km | +40.985 s |
| Recovered gravity-turn development inputs, `d=1.9 m` as Kn length | Kn=5 | 123.746 s | 133.0 km | 9.68 km | +26.646 s |
| Recovered gravity-turn development inputs, `d/2=0.95 m` as Kn length | Kn=5 | 120.949 s | 125.7 km | 9.68 km | +23.849 s |

The **max-q result is encouraging**: all reconstructed cases put maximum dynamic pressure around 9.7-10.0 km, close to the approximately 9 km described in the thesis. The transition-time discrepancy is therefore not explained simply by a gross failure of the lower-atmosphere trajectory.

## Knudsen-threshold sensitivity

The recovered atmosphere routine classified flow as continuum for `Kn <= 0.01`, transitional for `0.01 < Kn < 10`, and free molecular for `Kn >= 10`. The thesis algorithm, separately, states that gravity turn ends at `Kn = 5`.

Using the recovered Vega gravity-turn development inputs and the thesis interpretation of characteristic length as radius gives:

| Kn threshold | Time | Altitude | Error vs 97.1 s |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.01 | 104.087 s | 85.3 km | +6.987 s |
| 0.03 | 106.949 s | 91.8 km | +9.849 s |
| 0.1 | 109.964 s | 98.8 km | +12.864 s |
| 0.3 | 112.709 s | 105.3 km | +15.609 s |
| 1 | 115.954 s | 113.2 km | +18.854 s |
| 3 | 119.293 s | 121.5 km | +22.193 s |
| 5 | 120.949 s | 125.7 km | +23.849 s |
| 10 | 123.746 s | 133.0 km | +26.646 s |

The fact that `Kn=0.01` is much closer to the reported time is **not sufficient evidence** that the final 2014 program used that threshold. It is, however, a useful hypothesis because `0.01` is the continuum/transition boundary in the recovered atmosphere implementation.

## Provenance discrepancies that remain open

Several documented/recovered values do not fully agree:

- the dissertation specifies `Kn=5`, while the recovered atmosphere implementation explicitly classifies regime changes at `Kn=0.01` and `Kn=10`;
- the dissertation says the Kn characteristic length is the **last-stage radius**, while the recovered atmosphere function parameter is named `diam_last`;
- Table 6.1 gives P80 thrust as **2092 kN**, while the recovered Vega gravity-turn development test uses **2440 kN**;
- the recovered Vega trajectory test uses a **1.9 m** aerodynamic diameter, not the 3.0 m P80 diameter in Table 6.1;
- the surviving development test uses different propellant and initial masses from the published validation table.

These differences are preserved rather than silently reconciled.

## Decision

The reconstructed thesis model will continue to use **Kn=5 and last-stage radius** as the default historical specification because those choices are stated explicitly in the dissertation.

Alternative interpretations remain available only as validation/sensitivity cases. No coefficient or threshold will be selected merely because it makes the result closer to 97.1 s.
